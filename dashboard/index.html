Models & exploratory scenarios

Fixed evaluation · independent of filters

Five-year scenarios extrapolate historical configuration listings, not consumer demand. The newest observed model year is excluded from training and evaluation. Forecasts have no calibrated uncertainty intervals. See the project report for baseline comparisons and limitations.

Read the data carefully. The latest model years may be incomplete. Averages are not sales-weighted. The source snapshot contains model years beyond the calendar year. Statistical associations do not establish policy effects.
